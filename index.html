01 / About

How I think about
technology.

The part of my job I enjoy most is taking something complicated and figuring out how to make it clearer.

My background started in business and operations and grew into business systems analysis, portfolio enablement and enterprise technology. That combination has shaped how I approach problems: I care about the reason behind a request, but I also want to understand how the technology works and what it will mean for the people using it.

These days, a lot of my work is around Atlassian platforms, AI enablement, automation, security, governance and integrations. I enjoy the middle ground — talking with stakeholders, working with technical teams and translating between the two.
